# 🎬 Prompt Generation — Karakter "Si Juragan" (Hands-Only)
### Juragan by Anak Bawang

> Dokumen ini berisi prompt siap-pakai untuk AI image/video generator (Veo 3 Lite, Midjourney, Runway, Kling AI, Luma, dsb) supaya karakter tangan "Si Juragan" konsisten di setiap konten.

---

## 🔒 LOCKED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION BLOCK

Gunakan blok ini di **setiap prompt**, tempel di awal, baru tambahkan aksi/scene spesifik di bawahnya.

```
A pair of clean, well-groomed male hands and forearms (Indonesian/Southeast Asian skin tone,
light-medium tan), belonging to a trustworthy Central Javanese man in his early-to-mid 30s.
Nails short, trimmed, and clean. Skin looks healthy, slightly weathered from farm/kitchen work
but not rough or dirty. No jewelry except a simple thin black or brown analog watch on the left wrist.
Sleeves rolled up to mid-forearm, made of plain solid-color cotton t-shirt in earth tone
(cream, warm brown, or sage green) OR a clean canvas kitchen apron in cream/khaki with
a small subtle "Anak Bawang" logo patch.
Movements are slow, deliberate, calm, and purposeful — never rushed or jittery.
Face and body NOT shown — camera frames only hands, forearms, and the product on the table.
Setting: natural wood table OR clean stainless steel packing table OR cream linen table cloth,
softly lit with warm natural daylight (golden-hour warm tone, soft shadows, no harsh flash).
Background slightly blurred, minimal, clean kitchen or home-packing environment,
subtle warm color grading (amber/cream tones), shallow depth of field, cinematic but homey feel.
Products in frame: stand-up ziplock pouch of "Juragan Bawang Goreng" (label clearly visible,
facing camera when placed down), digital kitchen scale, fresh shallots (bawang merah) as props.
Overall mood: honest, warm, calm, trustworthy — like a documentary about an artisan producer,
not a flashy commercial. Cinematic 4K, soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field,
35mm or 50mm lens look, slight film grain, warm color grade.
```

## 🎥 VARIAN PROMPT PER JENIS SHOT

### 1️⃣ Hook Shot — Tangan Menunjuk / Menimbang
```
[LOCKED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION BLOCK]
Action: hands slowly placing a competitor's plastic jar of fried shallots onto a digital
kitchen scale, index finger gently tapping the scale screen to zero it out. Camera close-up,
top-down angle (flat lay / overhead shot), slight motion blur on fingertip movement,
slow-motion 50fps feel. Warm side lighting casting soft shadow of the jar.
```

### 2️⃣ Edukasi / Isi — Membuka Pouch & Menunjukkan Tekstur
```
[LOCKED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION BLOCK]
Action: hands carefully opening the ziplock seal of the Juragan Bawang Goreng stand-up pouch,
tilting it slightly so crispy fried shallots spill gently into a small clean white ceramic bowl.
Close-up 3/4 angle, camera at 30-degree angle above table, shallow focus on the shallots texture
(golden brown, crispy, dry — not oily or clumped). Steam-free, appetizing macro detail shot.
```

### 3️⃣ Perbandingan Berat — Timbang Bawang di Timbangan Digital
```
[LOCKED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION BLOCK]
Action: hands pouring fried shallots from the pouch directly onto the digital scale,
steady and precise motion, camera locked-off top-down shot showing scale numbers clearly
readable (250g), other hand holding the empty pouch beside it for size comparison.
Clean stainless steel table surface, neutral bright but warm lighting for scale readability.
```

### 4️⃣ Behind The Scene — Packing & Sealing
```
[LOCKED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION BLOCK]
Action: hands wearing a cream canvas apron, using an impulse sealer machine to seal the
stand-up pouch shut, steam/heat line visible for a split second, then hand smooths the sealed
edge flat. Camera slightly wider shot (medium close-up) to include part of the packing table
with a few finished pouches neatly stacked in the background, softly blurred.
```

### 5️⃣ Closing Shot — Menaruh Produk dengan Rapi
```
[LOCKED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION BLOCK]
Action: hands gently placing the sealed Juragan Bawang Goreng pouch upright on the wooden
table, label facing directly at camera, adjusting it slightly with two fingers until centered
and straight. Camera static, eye-level with the product (low angle), soft warm backlight
creating a subtle glow behind the pouch, cinematic product-hero framing.
Text overlay space reserved at bottom third of frame for closing tagline.
```

## 🎨 TEXT OVERLAY / SUBTITLE STYLE (untuk post-production)

```
Font: bold sans-serif, high readability on mobile
Warna utama: warm yellow (#F5C842) atau putih dengan background semi-transparan
Warna aksen/highlight: burnt orange (#C4622D)
Posisi: lower third atau center-bottom, dengan sedikit drop shadow agar terbaca di semua background
```

---

## 🚫 NEGATIVE PROMPT (Hindari)

```
No visible face, no full body, no visible mouth/head, no cartoonish or CGI-looking hands,
no dirty or greasy hands, no long or painted nails, no flashy jewelry/rings, no harsh flash
lighting, no cluttered/messy background, no cold blue color grading, no fast jittery camera
shake, no oily/soggy-looking fried shallots, no competitor logos visible, no low-resolution
or blurry product label text.
```

---

## ⚙️ TECHNICAL PARAMETERS (Veo 3 Lite / video gen)

```
Resolution: 4K atau minimal 1080p vertical (9:16) untuk TikTok/Reels
Frame rate: 24-30fps normal, 50-60fps untuk momen slow-motion (tekstur bawang, sealing)
Camera style: handheld-stabilized look, subtle natural micro-movement (bukan tripod kaku,
tapi juga bukan goyang)
Lighting: warm natural daylight simulation, soft key light dari samping/atas 45 derajat,
fill light lembut, tanpa bayangan keras
Color grade: warm/amber tone, sedikit film grain, contrast medium-soft (bukan HDR tajam)
Duration per shot: 3-6 detik per klip untuk hook/isi, 5-10 detik untuk closing
```

## 📝 CATATAN PENGGUNAAN

- Selalu tempel **LOCKED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION BLOCK** di awal prompt, baru tambahkan Action sesuai varian shot yang dibutuhkan.
- Jika generator butuh prompt lebih pendek (limit karakter), gunakan versi ringkas ini sebagai fallback:

```
Clean male hands (SE Asian skin tone), short trimmed nails, rolled-up earth-tone sleeves or
cream apron, calm deliberate movements, warm natural daylight, wooden/stainless table,
shallow depth of field, cinematic warm color grade, no face/body shown, holding/showing
Juragan Bawang Goreng stand-up pouch with label facing camera.
```

- Simpan semua output video mentah dengan penamaan konsisten, misal: `juragan_hook_01.mp4`, `juragan_isi_texture_01.mp4`, `juragan_closing_01.mp4` — supaya mudah disusun ulang di editor.
