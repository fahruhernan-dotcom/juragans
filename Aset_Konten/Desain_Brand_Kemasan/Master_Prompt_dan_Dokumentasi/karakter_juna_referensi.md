## 🤲 Spesifikasi Tangan & Gesture

```text
Hands-only framing, no face visible. Clean male hands with short trimmed nails. 
Movements are always slow, deliberate, calm, and purposeful — never rushed, 
shaky, or jerky. When holding a pouch, the grip is firm yet gentle. When pouring 
fried shallots, the motion is smooth and controlled. When pointing at product 
texture differences, the gesture is confident and unhurried.
```

---

## 👕 Wardrobe / Kostum

```text
Earth-tone casual shirt (warm brown, olive, or muted terracotta) with sleeves 
rolled up to forearm. Alternatively, a clean cream or olive canvas apron over 
a simple earth-tone t-shirt. No flashy accessories, no watch — clean and 
authentic artisan look.
```

---

## 🏠 Setting / Lingkungan

```text
Warm, authentic Boyolali home kitchen or clean packing room. Natural golden-hour 
sunlight streaming through a window. Visible elements: wooden table, digital 
weighing scale, ziplock pouches, raw red shallots, ceramic bowls of fried 
shallots. The environment conveys an honest local entrepreneur who prioritizes 
pure quality ingredients without additives.
```

## 🔒 Locked Character Prompt (Tempelkan ke Setiap Prompt Flow/Veo)

Salin blok teks di bawah ini dan tempelkan di awal setiap prompt video/gambar yang melibatkan karakter Juna:

```text
Character "Juna": A young male Indonesian artisan food producer from Boyolali. 
Camera shows ONLY hands and forearms (hands-only, no face). Clean hands with 
short trimmed nails. Movements are always slow, deliberate, calm, and purposeful. 
Wearing an earth-tone shirt with rolled sleeves or a cream canvas apron. Setting 
is a warm authentic home kitchen or clean packing room with golden-hour natural 
light. The character conveys an honest, quality-focused local entrepreneur.
```

---

## 📌 Cara Penggunaan di Google Flow

1. Buka **Google Flow** → Pilih karakter **Juna** (sudah tersimpan dengan voice Charon).
2. Saat membuat scene baru, **tempelkan Locked Character Prompt** di atas ke bagian awal prompt.
3. Lanjutkan dengan prompt aksi spesifik (misal: menuang bawang, menimbang, menunjuk tekstur, dll).
4. Pastikan framing selalu **hands-only** untuk konsistensi.

## 🎬 Contoh Prompt Kombinasi (Karakter + Aksi)

### Contoh 1: Juna Menuang Bawang ke Timbangan
```text
Character "Juna": hands-only, no face. Clean male hands with short nails, 
wearing cream canvas apron, rolled sleeves. Slow deliberate motion. 

Scene: Cinematic close-up of Juna's hands gently pouring crispy golden fried 
shallots from a frosted "JURAGAN" pouch onto a digital weighing scale. The 
scale reads 100g. Golden-hour natural window light, warm tones, shallow depth 
of field. 4K, 10 seconds.
```

### Contoh 2: Juna Menunjuk Perbedaan Grade
```text
Character "Juna": hands-only, no face. Earth-tone shirt, rolled sleeves. 
Calm purposeful gestures.

Scene: Top-down shot of Juna's hands placed between two small ceramic bowls — 
one filled with Grade Murni (lighter golden) and one with Grade A (deeper amber) 
fried shallots. His index finger slowly points from one to the other, then picks 
up a single flake to show texture. Warm kitchen setting, natural light, 4K, 8 seconds.
```
