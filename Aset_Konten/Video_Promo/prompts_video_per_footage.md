# Panduan Prompt AI Video Bawang Goreng Juragan

Dokumen ini berisi daftar prompt video siap-salin dalam bahasa Inggris (karena AI Video Generator seperti Kling, Luma, Runway Gen-3, dan Sora merespons prompt bahasa Inggris jauh lebih baik).

Setiap prompt dirancang dengan deskripsi kamera, pencahayaan, gerakan adegan, dan detail produk agar hasilnya konsisten, premium, dan sesuai dengan *brand identity* Juragan Bawang Goreng.

---

## ⚙️ Parameter & Pengaturan AI Video Generator (Rekomendasi)

* **Aspect Ratio (Rasio):** `9:16` (Vertikal untuk TikTok, Instagram Reels, dan YouTube Shorts)
* **Format Generasi:**
  * Jika memakai model yang mendukung input gambar (Image-to-Video / I2V), gunakan gambar kemasan/produk asli Anda sebagai referensi awal untuk bagian akhir (The Serving).
  * Jika memakai Text-to-Video (T2V), salin prompt di bawah ini secara utuh.
* **Kualitas/Resolusi:** `1080p` atau `4K` (High Quality)
* **Motion/Kekuatan Gerakan:** Sedang (`Medium` atau level `5-7` dari 10) untuk menghindari distorsi visual (efek meleleh/aneh).

## 🎥 OPSI A: Prompts Per Part (5 Bagian Utama - Durasi 5-9 Detik)

*Gunakan opsi ini jika Anda ingin menghemat kuota generasi dan ingin AI menggabungkan dua adegan langsung dalam satu part.*

### 🌾 Part 1: The Farm (Durasi Target: 5 Detik)

> **Prompt:**
> A cinematic transitions from a wide shot of a lush, vibrant green shallot farm under a warm, glowing morning sunrise, to a close-up shot of a local farmer's hand gently pulling a fresh bunch of red shallots with soil on their roots straight out of the rich dark ground. Highly realistic, warm golden lighting, shallow depth of field, 4k resolution, appetizing natural colors.
>
> **Panduan Bahasa Indonesia:**
> Transisi sinematik dari pemandangan luas sawah bawang yang hijau subur di bawah sinar matahari pagi hangat, ke bidikan dekat tangan petani mencabut bawang merah segar yang masih ada tanahnya dari tanah.

### 🧅 Part 2: The Peeling (Durasi Target: 5 Detik)

> **Prompt:**
> A smooth transition starting with a medium shot of a large rustic woven basket filled with fresh shallots arriving at a clean, professional stainless-steel kitchen. It cuts smoothly to a macro close-up of a worker's clean hands carefully peeling the dry papery red skin off a fresh shallot using a small pairing knife. Pristine commercial kitchen environment, soft lighting, sharp focus on the hands, hyper-realistic details.
>
> **Panduan Bahasa Indonesia:**
> Transisi mulus dari karung/keranjang anyaman berisi bawang merah segar yang tiba di dapur stainless steel bersih, lalu beralih ke close-up tangan pekerja mengupas kulit ari bawang merah kering dengan pisau kecil.

### 💦 Part 3: Wash & Slice (Durasi Target: 8 Detik)

> **Prompt:**
> A sequence of two shots: first, a close-up of fresh red shallots being washed under a stream of sparkling clean running water, water droplets splashing beautifully. Then, a smooth cut to a macro shot of a chef's hands using a sharp knife to slice the peeled shallots into thin, perfectly even rings on a rustic wooden cutting board. High-speed camera, crisp water motion, satisfying culinary preparation style, natural lighting.
>
> **Panduan Bahasa Indonesia:**
> Urutan adegan bawang dicuci di bawah air mengalir bersih dengan percikan air estetik, dilanjutkan dengan irisan pisau koki memotong bawang tipis-tipis di atas telenan kayu.

### 🔥 Part 4: The Sizzle (Durasi Target: 9 Detik)

> **Prompt:**
> A cinematic close-up of raw sliced shallots being dropped into a large pan of bubbling hot cooking oil, creating a satisfying sizzle and steam. The camera slowly pans and transitions into a slow-motion shot of a metal strainer scooping up perfectly crispy, golden-brown fried shallots as they are lifted out of the hot oil. Steam rising, warm appetizing colors, glistening oil, crisp detail, gourmet cooking documentary style.
>
> **Panduan Bahasa Indonesia:**
> Close-up bawang iris masuk ke minyak panas mendesis, dilanjutkan dengan serokan mengangkat bawang goreng berwarna kuning keemasan garing dari minyak secara slow-motion.

### 🍱 Part 5: The Serving (Durasi Target: 8 Detik)

> **Prompt:**
> Close-up of crispy golden fried shallots being poured into a frosted translucent matte standing pouch with a ziplock. The pouch has an elegant dark green and gold sticker label on the front with the brand name "JURAGAN" in gold text. The pouch is sealed. Then, transitions to a macro shot of the crispy, golden-brown shallots being sprinkled onto a steaming bowl of hot white rice, steam gently rising. Premium food styling, cinematic warm lighting, slow-motion, 4k.
>
> **Panduan Bahasa Indonesia:**
> Bawang goreng renyah dituang ke dalam pouch plastik buram (frosted) berstiker hijau-emas "JURAGAN", kemasan ditutup ziplock, lalu bawang ditaburkan di atas nasi putih panas mengepul.

### 1. Sawah Bawang (The Farm - Shot 1)

> **Prompt:**

> **Prompt:**
> Macro shot of a chef's hand slicing shallots into thin, uniform rings on a wooden cutting board with a sharp chef's knife. Precise cutting motion, culinary focus.

> **Prompt:**
> Close-up shot of sliced shallots dropping into a deep pan of hot, bubbling cooking oil, foaming and sizzling instantly. Warm lighting, steam rising.

> Slow motion shot of a steel strainer lifting crispy, golden-yellow fried shallots out of glistening hot oil. The oil drips off the crispy shallots. Warm gourmet color grading.

### 9. Memasukkan ke Pouch (The Serving - Shot 9)

> **Prompt:**
> Close-up of crispy golden fried shallots being poured into a frosted translucent matte standing pouch. The pouch has a dark green sticker label with a gold border on the front. Warm studio lighting.

### 10. Menabur di Nasi (The Serving - Shot 10)

> **Prompt:**
> Macro slow-motion shot of crispy golden-brown fried shallots being sprinkled onto a hot steaming bowl of fluffy white rice. Steam rising, delicious food styling.

## 💡 Tips Kombinasi & Editing Akhir (Pos-Produksi)

1. **Latching / Beat Matching:** Potong footage pas pada ketukan musik (beat drop). Misalnya, transisi dari bawang mentah ke minyak mendesis (Part 4) sangat cocok ditempatkan pada ketukan musik yang lebih dinamis.
2. **Sound FX (ASMR):** AI Video generator sering kali menghasilkan video tanpa suara (silent). Pastikan Anda mendownload efek suara berlisensi bebas atau merekam suara asli saat:
   * *Ssshhh* minyak goreng mendesis.
   * *Krik-krik* bawang dihancurkan/ditabur.
   * *Tak-tak-tak* pisau memotong bawang di talenan kayu.
3. **Color Consistency:** Saat mengedit di CapCut/Premiere, gunakan filter hangat (*warm filter*) yang sama di seluruh klip agar warna hijau sawah dan kuning keemasan bawang goreng terlihat menyatu.
